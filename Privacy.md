# Privacy Policy for Weave

**Last Updated: May 2026**

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application **Weave** (`com.infinity.weaveapp`), developed by Harshvardhan Sharma. Weave respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the data you share with us.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

To provide our core features, account functionality, and a personalized community experience, Weave collects the following data:

* **Account Credentials (Email Address):** Collected during sign-up/login solely for account authentication, user verification, and secure account recovery.
* **User-Generated Content (UGC):** We collect and store data that you voluntarily create and upload within the app, including posts, text, images, videos (clips), community banners, and profile information. This data is transmitted to our secure servers to make your content visible to your communities as intended by the app's functionality.

---

## 2. Device Permissions Used

Weave requests specific device permissions to enable interactive features. These permissions are executed strictly with your explicit consent:

* **Camera Permission:** Used exclusively when you choose to capture real-time photos or videos to upload directly into your posts, clips, profile pictures, or community banners. No media is captured or transmitted in the background without your active input.

---

## 3. Data Protection and Third-Party Sharing

* **Data Security:** We implement standard, industry-reasonable security measures to protect your personal information and uploaded media from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
* **No Third-Party Selling:** We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data, email addresses, or user-generated media to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
* **Service Providers:** Technical data processing (such as secure user authentication or media hosting) is handled through standard infrastructure providers solely to maintain app operations.

---

## 4. User Control, Data Access, and Deletion

We believe in giving you full control over your digital footprint. Inside the Weave application, you can natively edit or permanently delete your user-generated content (posts, images, clips, and profile details) at any time. 

If you wish to completely delete your account and remove your associated email address from our authentication database, you may do so via the in-app settings or by contacting us directly at the support email listed below.
